The LoveQuay Republic

Check a citizen card.

Type the number from the front of the card, or scan the code on the back. The answer comes from the billing system, so it is current.

Eight characters after the LQ. Letters I, L, O and U never appear on a card, so they are read as 1, 1, 0 and V.

Hold the code on the back of the card inside the frame.

What a green answer means

That this card number exists and the subscription behind it is paid and active today. Nothing more.

It is not proof of identity, and it does not entitle the holder to anything at the water. Nobody needs a card to swim here.

What it never shows

No name, no email address, no account number. The card number is opaque on purpose, so this page cannot be used to collect @lovequay.com addresses.

Grey is not red

If the service cannot be reached, this page says so plainly rather than calling the card invalid. A card that cannot be checked is not the same as a card that is fake.

Haven't got one?

You don't need one.

Swimming is free and always will be — that is the whole point of the place. A card is for people who want to fund the work and hold an @lovequay.com address.

Friends of Love Quay

A 400-metre quay running west out of Toronto's Inner Harbour into clean, deep, open water. Not owned, maintained, or supervised by anyone. Swim at your own risk, and keep it beautiful.
