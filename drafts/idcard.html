Anyone may check this card.

Scan the code on the front, or type the number at lovequay.com/verify.html. It reads as valid for exactly as long as the subscription behind it is paid — which is why there is no expiry date printed anywhere on it. The check answers valid or not valid and nothing else: no name, no address, no holder.

Article One

It is prohibited to prohibit swimming. This card buys no right to the water that its holder did not already have, and gives its holder no priority over anyone else on the deck.

What this is

A receipt for an annual subscription and a name on a mailbox. It is not a licence, a permit, or a ticket.

If it stops working

The card reads as not valid once the subscription lapses. There is nothing else to cancel and nothing to return.
